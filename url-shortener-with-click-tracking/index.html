What Pycoder click tracking records

When a Pycoder link is followed, the service records a click event for analytics. The tracker can show the stored total click count and a 30-day activity window. It also groups visits into broad device categories such as desktop, mobile, tablet, bot or other.

When the browser supplies a referrer, Pycoder stores only the referrer hostname for the link analytics view. Direct visits or requests without referrer information are grouped separately. The public analytics feature does not intentionally store visitor IP addresses in the product analytics tables.

  • Total click count for the short link.
  • Daily click activity across the latest 30-day window.
  • Broad device-category breakdown.
  • Top referrer hostnames when referral information is available.

Why track a shortened link?

A short link is more useful when you can tell whether people actually open it. Click data can help you compare where a link is being shared, spot a sudden increase in activity and decide whether a campaign or post is attracting attention.

For campaign marketing, use the UTM Builder to add source, medium and campaign parameters to the destination first. Shorten the finished campaign URL afterward. That keeps the shared link compact while preserving the UTM parameters for the analytics platform on the destination site.

What the numbers do—and do not—mean

A click is a redirect request, not necessarily a unique person or a conversion. The same visitor can click more than once, automated systems can request links, and some browsers or messaging apps may hide referrer information. Use click totals as directional link activity rather than treating them as a perfect count of individual people.

For conversion measurement, combine short-link activity with analytics on the destination website. Pycoder helps with the top of the funnel: creating the compact link and showing how that link is being opened.
