Safety

Acceptable Use Policy

Pycoder links are for legitimate sharing and campaign use. Harmful or deceptive use is not permitted.

Prohibited uses

  • Phishing, credential theft, impersonation or deceptive login pages.
  • Malware, ransomware, unwanted software or instructions designed to compromise devices or accounts.
  • Spam, unsolicited bulk messaging or attempts to bypass platform anti-spam protections.
  • Fraud, scams, deceptive financial schemes or intentionally misleading destinations.
  • Content that violates applicable law or facilitates unlawful activity.
  • Copyright or trademark infringement where you do not have a lawful basis to share the material.
  • Attempts to overload, scrape, probe or interfere with the service or its infrastructure.
  • Creating redirect loops or using the service to evade a previously disabled destination.

Enforcement

Reported or detected links may be investigated and can be disabled while preserving enough information to investigate the report. Abuse controls can include rate limiting and destination blocking.

Report a link

If you encounter a suspicious Pycoder link, use the abuse reporting form. Please provide the short URL and the most accurate reason you can.
