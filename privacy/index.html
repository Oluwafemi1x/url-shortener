Trust & privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026. This page describes the data handled by the current public version of Pycoder URL Shortener.

What the service stores

When you create a short link, the service stores the destination URL, the generated or custom short code, the creation time and a click counter. These records are needed so the redirect continues to work after server restarts and deployments.

Link analytics

When a Pycoder short link is opened, the service can store the click timestamp, the referring website hostname when a browser provides one, and a broad device category such as desktop, mobile, tablet, bot or other. The product analytics database does not intentionally store a visitor's IP address.

Local browser history

The homepage can keep your recently created links in your browser's local storage. That history stays in your browser unless you clear it. It is separate from the server-side redirect records.

Abuse reports

If you report a link, the service stores the reported short URL, the reason you selected, any optional details you submit, the report status and the report time.

Infrastructure logs

Hosting and infrastructure providers may process ordinary operational information such as IP addresses, request headers, timestamps and error logs as part of operating their services. Their handling of that information is governed by their own policies.

Cookies and accounts

The current version does not require an account to shorten links and does not use account-based advertising cookies. This policy will be updated before materially different tracking or advertising features are introduced.

Security and retention

Direct public access to the production database is restricted. Data is kept for as long as reasonably needed to keep active links working, provide analytics, investigate abuse and operate the service. Records may be removed when links are disabled, unsafe, obsolete or no longer needed.

Changes

This policy may change as the product evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
