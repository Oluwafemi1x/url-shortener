Share one clear destination

A long URL with tracking parameters can be awkward in a WhatsApp message or social post. A short link gives readers a compact address while redirecting them to the same destination. Mention what the link opens so people can decide whether to follow it.

A practical campaign workflow

  1. Choose a public landing page that works on a phone.
  2. Use the UTM Builder to label each channel: for example, source whatsapp, medium message, campaign october_launch.
  3. Copy the complete tagged URL into the URL shortener.
  4. Open the result once to verify the destination and query parameters.
  5. Share it with a short description and use the Link Tracker to review opens.

Use separate links for different placements

To compare a WhatsApp broadcast with an Instagram profile, give each destination its own source tag before shortening. Reusing the same short link everywhere combines the click count and makes channel comparisons harder.

Will a shortened URL work in every caption?

A shortener cannot change a platform's link rules. Some placements may display a URL as plain text rather than a tappable link. Test the exact placement and use a permitted profile or message link where needed.

Why can a preview look different?

Messaging apps and social networks decide how they fetch and cache link previews. Shortening does not guarantee a specific title, picture or preview. Test your message before sending it widely.

Do clicks equal customers?

No. A click records an open of the short URL, and automated preview fetches or repeated visits can affect counts. Use destination analytics and completed enquiries or purchases to measure outcomes. See the click-tracking guide for interpretation.

Sharing a printed version

For a flyer or event handout, create a QR code from your tested short URL. Include a readable address beside it and scan the printed result before distribution.
